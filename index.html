We developed Laboratory Legend, an engaging VR game for Lannett CDMO that transforms pharmaceutical development into an interactive journey. Players guide a scientist through a series of cleverly designed challenges that mirror actual drug development stages, from initial discovery through clinical trials to commercialization.

The experience features 10 unique obstacles that educate players about pharmaceutical manufacturing while maintaining engaging gameplay. Created for CPHI Milan, this innovative approach helped the Lannett CDMO booth stand out by making complex pharmaceutical processes accessible and entertaining for tradeshow attendees.

This project reflects the kind of work I lead at Atlas Rosetta: pairing technical execution with a clear understanding of a client's audience, so the end result is both functional and memorable.

Orchard Road, Asia's world-class shopping district in Singapore, presents a stunning showcase of Christmas decorations every year. In 2021, our team had the privilege of working on this showcase, known across Asia as "Christmas on a Great Street". We crafted the campaign's storyline and visuals to work with the physical displays along the street, then built all of the campaign's marketing materials.

We also built the campaign's online presence: a microsite with interactive games and a 360° virtual drive-through of the street. It drew crowds from across the region and added a fun slant to the showcase.

Projects like this are what Atlas Rosetta specializes in: bringing technical and creative disciplines together so a client's vision translates cleanly from concept to a working, public-facing experience.
