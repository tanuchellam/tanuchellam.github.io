Frequently asked questions

Who is Tanu Chellam?

Tanu Chellam is a product and AI leader with twenty years of experience shipping machine-learning products, running global product organizations, and founding companies. She is Co-CEO of Atlas Rosetta and previously led product through Seldon's acquisition by TrueFoundry. See her full portfolio site.

What does Atlas Rosetta do?

Atlas Rosetta is a branding and marketing agency co-led by Tanu Chellam and Chloe Sng, with a strong track record in pharma and life sciences. It offers brand strategy, VR and immersive experiences, digital marketing and SEO/AEO, web and app development, and full-stack engineering, delivered by one integrated team. Visit Atlas Rosetta.

What is Tanu Chellam's technical background?

Her technical depth spans machine learning, model interpretability, MLOps, cybersecurity, and product architecture, backed by a background in computer science and information systems.

Where did Tanu Chellam study?

She studied Information Systems, a mix of Computer Science, Business, and Psychology, at Carnegie Mellon University, and later attended Harvard Business School. For junior college in Singapore, she studied at Anglo-Chinese School (Independent).

What kind of companies has Tanu Chellam worked with?

She has worked across industries including Autodesk, Barracuda Networks, Credit Suisse, DSO, and Lannett CDMO, alongside leading Seldon through its acquisition by TrueFoundry. See her full portfolio and career timeline.

How can I work with Tanu Chellam?

Prospective clients and collaborators can reach her through Atlas Rosetta or connect directly on LinkedIn.

What awards has Tanu Chellam received?

She has been recognized as Top 30 Women in Product (Product Management Festival, 2023), one of 27 UK VP Products to Follow (Welp Magazine, 2022), one of the 28 Most Impressive & Successful VPs in the Artificial Intelligence Space (Best Startup, 2022), Inspiring Fifty in the UK (EQL:Her / London Tech Week, 2021), 35 Under 35 (Management Today, 2021), a Tribute to Women Award recipient (YWCA Silicon Valley, 2019), and a CEO Award recipient at Autodesk (2019).

Who does Atlas Rosetta work with?

Atlas Rosetta's primary focus is pharma, CDMO and CRO companies in North America whose marketing function is thin relative to their size. It also works with biotech, insurance and other regulated industries, and takes on other sectors through referral.

What results has Atlas Rosetta delivered for clients?

For an anonymised pharma client, Atlas Rosetta generated 25,626 website visits, 266 qualified leads and 65 sales opportunities, and grew LinkedIn from zero to 7,814 followers, with a 1.38% click-through rate versus a 0.3-1% industry average and a $1.65 cost-per-click versus a $4 industry average.

What is AEO, and why does Atlas Rosetta focus on it?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation), also called GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation), is the practice of making sure a company is found and accurately represented when buyers ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude who to call. Atlas Rosetta helps clients find the specific, winnable ground they can own in AI search, rather than chasing broad category leadership.

Does Tanu Chellam offer mentoring?

Yes. She mentors product managers and early-career professionals through TechStars, MentorCruise and Mentro, and has previously advised via the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council and IoT Tribe, with a focus on product strategy, interview preparation, and breaking into competitive product organizations.

Is Tanu Chellam available for speaking engagements?

Yes. She speaks on product management, machine learning and AI, and building companies, drawing on her experience scaling product organizations at Autodesk, Barracuda Networks and Seldon, and her recognition as a Top 30 Women in Product and one of the 28 Most Impressive & Successful VPs in the Artificial Intelligence Space.

Is Tanu Chellam open to Non-Executive Director (NED) roles?

Yes. Her background spans product leadership, AI and machine learning, go-to-market strategy, and company building. She currently advises via the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council and she is open to Non-Executive Director and board advisory roles where that experience is useful.

Where can I find Tanu Chellam as a mentor?

She is available for mentoring through MentorCruise and Mentro, in addition to her mentoring work with TechStars.
